India vs USA Job Market Trends, Demand and Salary Analysis

About the project

This  project is a Power BI dashboard where I compared the job market of India and the USA
from  January to June 2026. I used 100,009 job posting records from Fliar BI, which covers
around  15K  companies, 92 regions and 24 roles that are common in both countries. All the
findings below are based on this sample only, so they show what this data says and not the
whole job market.

Job demand

The USA has most of the postings in this data, about 80K jobs, while India has around 20K.
Among regions, California has the highest demand with 11,867 jobs. The lowest is Arunachal
Pradesh  with  just  1  job,  which  shows  how  uneven  the  postings are across regions.
Healthcare  is the most demanded role with 13,909 jobs and Devops-Cloud is the lowest with
556 jobs. While it can be viewed using slicer above for filering India and US

Monthly trends

Job  postings  were highest in January for the USA and then went down till March, which is
the  lowest  month overall with 12,553 jobs. After that it started going up again and June
became  the  highest  month  with 23,562 jobs. The USA postings kept falling from 19.6K in
January to around 10K in May, but India kept growing every month.

India vs USA comparison

This  was  the  most  interesting  part  for  me. India started with almost no postings in
January  (0.0K)  and  reached  10.5K  in June, while the USA was 13.1K in June. So the gap
became  very  small by the end. In job share, India went from near 0% to about 45% in June
and  the  USA  came  down from 100% to about 55%. The month on month growth of India looks
very big in February (over 1000%), but this is mostly because it started from a very small
number, so I would not read too much into it.

Salary analysis

The  average  salary  in  the  data  is  $10.12K. The USA is the highest paying country at
$12,420 average, and India shows only about $0.9K, which is a very big difference. I think
this  might be partly because of how salary is recorded for Indian postings, so I would be
careful  before saying Indian jobs actually pay that low. Operations is the highest paying
role  and  Admin  is  the  lowest.  Experts  earn  more on average ($14.01K) than freshers
($10.07K),  but  the  salary  by  experience  chart goes up and down a lot, so there is no
smooth pattern. The monthly average salary also went up from $1.3K in January to $19.5K in
May and then dropped a little in June.

Dashboard and full research report

My  dashboard  only  uses  the  100,009  record  sample for January to June 2026. The full
research report may use more data or clean it in a different way, so some totals, rankings
and  percentages  can  look  a  bit  different from mine. This does not mean either one is
wrong, they are just based on different amounts of data. The whole analysis is done using Dollar($)
curreny unit not rupee.
